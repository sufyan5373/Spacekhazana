WHAT'S INSIDE

Every corner of the vault has a job.

Nothing here is decoration — each part of SpaceKhazana was built to solve something a normal cloud drive gets wrong.

Files, organized like they matter

Breadcrumb navigation, bulk selection, thumbnail previews, and chunked uploads up to 30 GB a file.

Music that follows you

Upload albums, register YouTube tracks, and cache everything offline the moment it lands in your library.

Browse & discover

Publish tracks to a shared Browse tab when you want them found — everything else stays private by default.

Built like a vault

Token-based auth and isolated storage per person — nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

Works everywhere

Installs as an app on iOS and Android and keeps working with spotty or no connection at all.

Command from anywhere

A dedicated admin panel gives full oversight of the platform — no guesswork, no digging through logs.

Never get caught off guard

A built-in notification bell watches your storage in the background and warns you before you hit your limit — no surprise "quota full" errors mid-upload.

A vault inside the vault

A dedicated password manager sits alongside your files, so the credentials for the rest of your life stay in the same private space you already trust.

BUILT BY ONE PERSON

Sufyan built this from nothing — twice.

SpaceKhazana is designed, coded, and run by its founder, Sufyan, from Islamabad. When the original server was lost, he rebuilt it from scratch by reverse-engineering his own interface, route by route, until every feature worked exactly as before — then kept going.

It now runs on his own infrastructure, tunneled straight from his machine to the world, with no team, no outsourcing, and no corner cut on the parts that matter: your files stay yours.

Every feature — the Music section's offline caching, the notification bell that watches your storage, the admin panel, the encrypted password vault — was built, broken, and rebuilt by hand until it felt right. SpaceKhazana isn't a product pitch; it's Sufyan's own daily-driver cloud, shared with anyone who wants the same thing.

— Sufyan
Founder · Designer · Engineer, SpaceKhazana
